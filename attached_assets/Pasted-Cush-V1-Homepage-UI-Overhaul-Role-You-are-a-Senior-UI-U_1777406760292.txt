Cush V1 Homepage & UI Overhaul
Role: You are a Senior UI/UX Designer specializing in High-Trust Fintech (e.g., Stripe, Mercury, or Revolut).

Objective: Redesign the Cush homepage and dashboard to reflect our new focus: The Global Credit Identity Layer. The design must communicate "Security," "Global Mobility," and "Financial Power."

1. Visual Identity & Theme:

Color Palette: - Base: Deep Slate/Dark Navy (#0f172a) for text and depth.

Action: Vibrant Trust Blue (#2563eb) for primary buttons.

Success: Emerald Green (#10b981) for verification badges and score increases.

Background: Use a clean, light-gray/white background with subtle gradients or a "mesh" effect to feel modern and airy.

Typography: Use a clean, high-readability sans-serif (Inter or Geist). Headlines should be bold with tight letter spacing.

2. Hero Section (Above the Fold):

The Headline: "Your Financial Reputation, Now Borderless."

The Sub-headline: "The Cush Passport turns your global income and assets into a verified credit score that travels with you. Prove your reliability to landlords and lenders—before or after you land."

Primary CTA: "Get Your Passport Score" (Primary Blue Button).

Secondary CTA: "View a Sample Passport" (Ghost/Outline Button).

Visual Asset: A high-fidelity mockup of the Cush Passport Card showing a score of 785, a "Verified" badge, and a "High Capacity" label.

3. The "Dual-Mode" Entry Point:

Create a two-column "Choose Your Journey" section:

Left Side: "Preparing to Move? (Pre-Arrival)" – Focus on building a score from home.

Right Side: "Recently Arrived? (Post-Arrival)" – Focus on bridging your global history to your new home.

4. The "Trust Signal" Feature Grid (3-Columns):

Icon 1 (Global Signal): "More than a Credit Score. We measure your 'Peak Capacity' and global asset history."

Icon 2 (Privacy First): "Bank-Grade Security. Share your score without exposing your raw bank transactions."

Icon 3 (Day Zero Ready): "Instant Trust. Send your verified link to landlords to bypass 6-month rent deposits."

5. Dashboard UI (Post-Login):

The Score Center: A large, circular gauge or a clean "Premium Card" design displaying the user's current Cush Score.

The Roadmap (Checklist): A sleek, interactive sidebar or list showing "Ways to Boost Your Score." Items should look like "tasks" with +10/+20 point indicators.

The Share Panel: A prominent section with a "Copy Public Link" button and a toggle to "Revoke Access" at any time.

6. Mobile Responsiveness:

Ensure the Passport Card looks like a physical card on mobile (Apple Wallet style).

Buttons must be full-width and "thumb-friendly" on mobile devices.

Constraint: Avoid generic stock photos. Use clean SVG icons, high-quality component shadows, and data-driven visualizations.

Success Tip for the AI Agent:
When you run this, tell the agent: "Use Lucide-React for icons and Shadcn/UI for the components. Ensure the spacing follows a strict 4px/8px grid system for a 'tight' professional feel."
