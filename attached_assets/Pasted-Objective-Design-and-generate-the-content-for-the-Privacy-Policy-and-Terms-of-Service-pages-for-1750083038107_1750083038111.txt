Objective: Design and generate the content for the "Privacy Policy" and "Terms of Service" pages for the Cush platform. These pages must be clear, comprehensive, legally compliant, and user-friendly, reflecting Cush's specific services, data handling practices, and user responsibilities.

Core Requirements:
Clear Language & Accessibility:

Content should be written in clear, concise, and easy-to-understand language. Avoid excessive legal jargon where plain language suffices.

Ensure the pages are accessible and easily navigable for all users.

Compliance Focus:

Explicitly mention adherence to relevant global data protection regulations (e.g., GDPR for EU, CCPA for California, NDPR for Nigeria, PIPEDA for Canada, UK GDPR, and general principles for other jurisdictions).

Address compliance for financial services data and medical data (if ConnectCare/VitalLink is integrated in the future, currently placeholder/mention of general data types).

Dynamic Content Integration:

Design the content to be hosted on dedicated web pages (e.g., /privacy-policy, /terms-of-service) within the Cush frontend.

Structure the content in Markdown for easy rendering on the frontend.

Privacy Policy Page: Important Sections
This section needs to detail how Cush collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects user data.

Introduction:

State Cush's commitment to user privacy.

Briefly explain what Cush is (global immigration services, remittances, loans, etc.).

Information We Collect:

Personal Identifiable Information (PII):

User-provided: Name, email, phone number, address, date of birth, nationality, immigration status, financial details (bank accounts, card info - note: handled by Stripe), medical history (if health features are present), educational background.

Automatically collected: IP address, device information, browser type, usage data (pages visited, features used), session data, cookies.

Sensitive Data: Explicitly mention handling of sensitive data such as immigration status, nationality, and financial details.

Financial Data: Clarify that actual payment card details are handled by Stripe, and Cush does not store them directly.

Health Data (Conditional): If health features are enabled, explain the collection and special protection for health-related information (e.g., symptoms, diagnoses, medical records).

How We Use Your Information:

To provide and maintain services (user accounts, immigration assistance, remittances, loans).

For authentication and security (MFA, fraud prevention).

For personalization (AI assistant, tailored advice).

For communication (service updates, support, marketing with consent).

For legal and regulatory compliance.

For research and analytics (on aggregated, de-identified data for market insights, value-based care).

How We Share Your Information:

With Service Providers: Third-party vendors (Stripe, Twilio, Google/Gemini API, Drizzle ORM/PostgreSQL, cloud providers, KYC/AML providers, remittance partners, loan partners). Specify that they are bound by confidentiality.

With Your Consent: For example, sharing specific data with mentors or housing providers if explicitly authorized by the user.

For Legal Reasons: To comply with laws, respond to subpoenas, protect rights.

Business Transfers: In case of mergers or acquisitions.

Aggregated/De-identified Data: For research, analytics, and improving services without identifying individual users.

Data Security:

Detail measures taken: Encryption (at rest and in transit), access controls, regular security audits, employee training.

Reiterate that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Data Retention:

Explain how long data is kept and the criteria for retention (legal obligations, service provision).

Your Rights (Data Subject Rights):

Right to access personal data.

Right to rectification (correct inaccurate data).

Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten" - within legal limits).

Right to restrict processing.

Right to data portability.

Right to object to processing.

How to exercise these rights (contact information, in-app controls).

Cookies and Tracking Technologies:

Explain the use of cookies and similar technologies.

How users can manage cookie preferences.

International Data Transfers:

Address data transfers across borders, especially for global services (e.g., using Standard Contractual Clauses).

Children's Privacy:

State age restrictions and policies for minors.

Changes to This Privacy Policy:

Explain how users will be notified of changes.

Contact Us:

Provide clear contact information for privacy-related inquiries.

Terms of Service Page: Important Sections
This section outlines the rules and agreements between Cush and its users, setting expectations and responsibilities.

Introduction:

Welcome message, agreement to terms.

Defines parties involved (Cush, User).

Acceptance of Terms:

Explicit statement that by using the service, the user agrees to the terms.

Services Provided:

Detailed description of Cush's services (immigration assistance, mentorship, remittances, loans, AI chatbot, housing/jobs board, community features).

Clarify Cush's role (e.g., platform provider, referral agent, not a direct lender/employer/landlord for all services).

User Accounts:

Account creation, eligibility requirements (age, legal capacity).

Account security (user responsibility for password, MFA).

Account suspension/termination policy.

User Responsibilities:

Prohibited conduct (illegal activities, fraud, harassment, unauthorized access).

Accuracy of information provided by the user.

Compliance with applicable laws.

Use of AI chatbot (e.g., not for medical emergencies, AI limitations).

Intellectual Property Rights:

Ownership of Cush platform content, trademarks, and user-generated content.

User's license to Cush for submitted content.

Payments and Fees:

Pricing structure for services.

Refund policy.

Responsibilities for taxes.

Clarification of third-party payment processing (Stripe).

Disclaimers and Limitation of Liability:

Crucial for mitigating risk.

"As Is" Basis: Services provided without warranties.

No Medical/Legal/Financial Advice: Explicitly state that Cush (and its AI) does not provide medical, legal, immigration, or financial advice. It's a platform for information and connection. Users must seek professional advice.

Third-Party Services: Disclaim liability for services provided by third-party partners (loan providers, remittance agents, doctors, landlords, employers).

Limitation of Liability: Capping Cush's liability for damages.

Indemnification:

Users agree to protect Cush from claims arising from their misuse of the platform.

Dispute Resolution:

Governing law (e.g., jurisdiction where Cush is registered).

Arbitration clause (if preferred over litigation).

Class action waiver.

Links to Third-Party Websites:

Disclaim responsibility for external sites.

Changes to the Terms of Service:

How and when terms may be updated.

Termination:

Conditions under which either party can terminate the agreement.

Miscellaneous:

Severability, entire agreement, waiver.

Contact Information:

How to contact Cush for terms-related inquiries.

Expected Deliverables:
frontend/src/pages/privacy-policy.md: Markdown content for the Privacy Policy page.

frontend/src/pages/terms-of-service.md: Markdown content for the Terms of Service page.

frontend/src/components/LegalLinks.tsx (Conceptual): A simple React component to display links to these pages in the footer or navigation.
