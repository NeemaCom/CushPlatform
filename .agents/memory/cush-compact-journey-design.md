---
name: Cush compact journey design
description: User-stated design direction for Cush product interfaces.
---

New Cush interfaces should feel compact, inviting, and journey-led rather than presenting long, dense forms all at once. Reveal relevant inputs progressively and preserve a clear sense of the user's current stage.

**Why:** The user asked for a calmer, minimalist approach to reduce busy screens and form overload, and said to apply it to future Cush implementations.

**How to apply:** Use this direction for future Cush UI work while preserving existing product behavior and data. For Passport, distinguish Pre-arrival from Post-arrival and keep the Score and Confidence concepts clear and separate.
